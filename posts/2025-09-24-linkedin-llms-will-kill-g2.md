# LLMs will kill G2

## Hook

## Post

> LLMs will kill G2
>
> For years, most SaaS decisions relied on G2 (Trustpilot for B2B)
>
> But let’s be honest:
> - Rankings are biased by who pays more for visibility.
> - Reviews are often cherry-picked, fake, or outdated.
> - The “best” software is rarely what you find on the first page.
>
> And yet, this model drives $25+ billions in revenue for software companies every year.
>
> But here’s the thing: AI will break this.
>
> Tomorrow (today), when you will look for a software, you won’t open G2 anymore.
>
> You will ask Chatgpt or Perplexity: “What’s the best CRM for a 50-person team in the US?”
>
> The LLM will query the entire web, synthesize facts, and deliver context-based recommendations. Better than anything.
>
> That’s what we’ve built saas-ranking.ai: A ranking of the best SaaS solutions, powered by LLMs.
>
> No pay-to-play.
>
> No fake reviews.
>
> Just facts, presence, and relevance in LLMs.
>
> In short: objective rankings for the AI era.
>
> We're live on Product Hunt with it now! It's 100% free, for the community and made to evolve.
>
> Make sure to upvote 🙏
