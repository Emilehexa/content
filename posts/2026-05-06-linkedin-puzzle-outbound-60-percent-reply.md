# Everyone is racing to build the best AI outbound machine.

## Hook

> We did the opposite and 60% reply rate.

## Post

> Everyone is racing to build the best AI outbound machine.
> We did the opposite and 60% reply rate.
>
> When you're early, outbound is a mess: No brand. A new product nobody had heard of - you have to play a different game.
>
> So we decided to kill volume completely.
>
> Instead, we sent physical puzzles to some of the hottest companies in the Bay.
>
> Each puzzle was their logo - with one missing piece. That missing piece?
>
> Rose (HX 25): An AI agent that talks to your website visitors and converts them.
>
> Results: 50% of replies turned into meetings
>
> Why it worked? It's a human to human in world full of AI.
>
> And very soon, you will need to sell to the AI agents and this won't work anymore.
>
> But for now - I only have one advice: spread mountains of puzzles around SF (and invest a bit in GEO - this might help in the future)
>
> PS: We cut puzzle build time from 30 to 16 min. Scaling the unscalable 👀
