## Post

>
> One thing I've learned from working with founders is that before product-market fit, there's founder-market fit.
>
> The best companies often emerge when a founder sits at the intersection of three things:
> - a deep understanding of a problem.
> - a unique set of experiences.
> - a genuine ability to create value for customers.
>
> That's why seeing Lydia Bellahouel on OpenAI's stage this week felt so right.
>
> Over the past months, I've had a front-row seat to the journey of Verso (HX26). And what has always stood out to me is how naturally Lydia fits this market.
>
> Consumer insights isn't just a category she chose. It's a problem she understands deeply after 7 years at BCG. She knows how critical consumer understanding is for decision-making, and how frustratingly slow, expensive, and inaccessible traditional research can be.
>
> What's impressive is that she's now building exactly where her strengths, experience, and customer obsession overlap.
>
> The result is Verso: a company helping brands get consumer insights in days instead of weeks, already working with 30+ clients including Fortune 500 companies only a few months after launch.
>
> Watching the broader ecosystem discover Verso through OpenAI's event was exciting.
>
> Because it's another reminder that when founder-market fit is exceptional, things that look impossible from the outside start to feel inevitable.
>
> Congrats to Lydia Bellahouel, Camille Epitalon, Maxime Bascon, Mathis Anselme, Thomas Tabouelle and the Hexa team Florent Quinti, Pierre Lemaire & Nelly Seiglan
>
> PS: Verso is also one of the most AI native company I know.
