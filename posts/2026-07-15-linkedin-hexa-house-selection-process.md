## Post

> Silicon Valley has a type it likes to fund: relentless, abrasive, closer to sociopath than nice guy.
>
> We bet on the opposite - and we were just as selective doing it.
>
> 38 interviews. 19 hours straight. Under 72 hours. That's what it took to go from 250+ applications to 10 seats in the house.
>
> The DMs alone were in the hundreds - people I'd never met, warm intros, cold pitches, all landing the same week.
>
> Everyone who applied Monday knew by Sunday if they were moving to SF.
>
> Three filters mattered:
> - Background - what they'd actually built or achieved before this
> - Speed of execution - how fast they'd moved on a raw idea to concrete
> - Whether I'd want to work with them every day
>
> That last one isn't consensus in the Valley. This cohort has backgrounds that don't look like anything standard - some of them think in ways that are neurodivergent, wired completely differently from each other.
>
> But every one of them is someone I actually want to have dinner with.
>
> Two weeks. That's how long it took to build the strongest and coolest founder house in SF.
