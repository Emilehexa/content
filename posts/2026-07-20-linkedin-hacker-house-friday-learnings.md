## Post

> I host a hacker house in SF (Hexa ❤️) with founders from MIT, YC, exited...
>
> Every Friday, the founders in the house share what they learned that week. Three things came up last week.
>
> Warm beats cold.
>
> A YC partner told one of the teams to exhaust their close network before touching cold outreach. People open up differently with someone they already trust. That's where the real problems show up, the ones you're actually trying to solve.
>
> Talk 10% of the call.
>
> Same team flipped their discovery calls this week. Less talking. More recording. One founder said he learned more in 20 minutes of silence than in three months of pitching.
>
> Niche down to compete head on.
>
> Legora didn't launch as "Harvey for Europe." Harvey already owned that story. Legora went after the largest law firms in Europe instead, a segment Harvey wasn't fighting for. They compete with Harvey directly now. Globally.
>
> This isn't new advice. It just hits differently once you've lived it.
