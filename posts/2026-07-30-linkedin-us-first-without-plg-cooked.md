# If you want to be US first without being PLG and being European, you're mostly cooked.

## Hook

> If you want to be US first without being PLG and being European, you're mostly cooked.

## Post

> If you want to be US first without being PLG and being European, you're mostly cooked.
>
> Lovable, ElevenLabs, Vibe.co, FullEnrich, viktor.com... They all started like that.
>
> After some time spend on the ground, here are the three main reasons:
>
> No credibility yet: At early stage nobody has a track record to point to. As a European founder it's worse: no US school on your resume, no network here, none of the patterns that usually reassure an a buyer.
>
> Outbound saturation: Every US inbox is already full of SDRs running a talk track that doesn't quite land (everyone has also a parrallel dialer since 4 years here).
>
> Time to value has to be short: It's the ChatGPT era, but it's also the culture here.
> Sales-led just isn't a fair fight for an early stage European founder here anymore.
>
> Curious if anyone has a real counter-example: a European company that broke into the US from scratch on sales-led, no PLG motion at all.
>
> Drop it below.

## Call to action
