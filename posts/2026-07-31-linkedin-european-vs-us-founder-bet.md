# If I had to choose between investing in a US founder or a European founder in San Francisco, I'd take the European every time.

## Hook

> If I had to choose between investing in a US founder or a European founder in San Francisco, I'd take the European every time.

## Post

> If I had to choose between investing in a US founder or a European founder in San Francisco, I'd take the European every time.
>
> San Francisco is the single most competitive tech market on earth. For an American founder, being here is just being home. No visa to sort out, no need to rebuild a network from zero.
>
> Plenty show up simply because it's the place to be, not because they proved anything to get here.
>
> For a European founder, none of that applies. No safety net, no home turf to fall back on when things get hard.
>
> The ones who aren't strong enough don't last. The ones who do are operating at the top of the game, because that's the only way they survived it.
>
> So the math flips. Talk to a European founder in SF and you're very likely talking to someone tier 1. Talk to an American founder and you might be, or you might just be talking to someone who happened to grow up nearby.
>
> If you're trying to bet on the strongest founders in this city, the Europeans in the room are a much better bet than the room average suggests.
