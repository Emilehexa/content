# If I had to bet on founders in SF, I would bet every time on Europeans.

## Hook

> If I had to bet on founders in SF, I would bet every time on Europeans.

## Post

> If I had to bet on founders in SF, I would bet every time on Europeans.
>
> This summer we launched a hacker house program with Hexa in SF.
>
> No equity, no money, no contract. Our only goal - attract the best talents.
>
> We got 250 applications in 1 week for 9 companies to join. We even got the support from OpenAI & Rho.
>
> And one month later, the outcomes are stellar:
> - 5 companies have been accepted to the YC F26 batch (83% of applications)
> - Most of the projects succeed in their US GTM and some closing more than $100k ARR
> - One company even got funded by tier1 investors
>
> I do believe in European entrepreneurship.
>
> I also do believe that if you're ambitious, you must come to SF, to optimize the odds to build the next generational company.
>
> That's our vision with Hexa in SF. Bring the best founders in the best place to build.
>
> We are not a YC launchpad. We want to build something different. Much bigger.
>
> And we’re going to need you.
>
> More tomorrow. Stay tuned:)
