# Audience

Who the posts are for.

Source: Emile's answers (2026-11-19).

## Primary readers

1. **Founders based in San Francisco.**

## Open questions

- **Stage and sector:** which founder stage (pre-seed, seed, Series A, ...) and sectors matter most?
- **What they want:** their goals, in their own words.
- **What keeps them stuck:** pains, frustrations, recurring questions.
- **What they already believe:** opinions to confirm or challenge.
- **Where they spend time:** beyond LinkedIn and X (communities, newsletters, events).
- **Secondary audiences:** investors, operators, partners? What should each take away?
- **Who this is not for.**
