# Editorial line

How Emile sounds and what he talks about. The editorial line is a funnel with three pillars. Every post gets one `pillar` and one `topic` in its frontmatter.

Source: Emile's answers (2026-11-19).

## Tone references

Three creators whose tone Emile likes (source: Emile's answers). Guillaume and Harry are LinkedIn references, read on 2026-11-20 through a public scrape and web search. Ben Cera is an X reference, pulled through the X API with public metrics on 2026-11-20.

### Guillaume Roux-Romestaing (LinkedIn)

[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/guillaumerx21/). Founder at Desk One, a16z scout, based in San Francisco, about 11,000 followers. Studied 13 of his posts from Apr to Nov 2026.

- **Hooks:** provocation ("If you're not a media company first, you're dead"), a money offer ("I want to send you $1k every month"), insider explainers ("How VC scout cheques really work"), a role-play opener ("POV: you just moved to SF as a European founder (part 1)"), a personal milestone ("8 months, and 1 AI Content Engine later - I now have 250,000 followers"), curiosity ("I've been wondering why so many great french founders are in Growth/GTM").
- **Structure:** two formats.
  - Short offer posts: hook, a few lines or a bullet list, then a direct call to action ("send us customers - we pay 10% rev-share").
  - Long listicles: a credibility line ("I scout startups for a16z"), then 4 or 5 numbered points with italic subheads ("*1. Sell it before you build it*"). Each point has a claim, a personal proof with names and numbers, and a takeaway. Ends on a Europe vs SF contrast ("Europe waits for you to be chosen. SF assumes you started last week.") and an ask ("add me on LinkedIn").
  - Bridge phrases: "Hear me out", "Here's how it actually runs", "Save this if...".
- **Length:** short posts 30 to 80 words; listicles 350 to 450 words.
- **Voice:** first person, casual, often lowercase, typos left in. Uses coined phrases ("trickle-down conte-nomics"), a repeated slogan ("attention is all you need"), absurd humour, credibility drops (a16z, YC, dollar amounts), tagged names, the occasional line in French and flag emojis. Topics overlap with Emile's: European founders in SF, GTM, founder advice.

### Harry Stebbings (LinkedIn)

[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/harrystebbings/). Founder of 20VC (podcast and venture fund), based in London, about 290,000 followers. Studied 13 of his posts from Oct 2025 to Nov 2026.

- **Hooks:** a bold claim with a percentage ("90% of the podcasts you hear on AI today are BS", "99% of them are former investment banker box tickers"), a result first ("36 hours after meeting Hugo Santos we agreed to invest $10M into Namespace."), an "uncomfortable truth" framing ("There are three ugly truths right now that we are not admitting."), a story teaser ("The story of Fractile is wild!"), frustration ("I am fed up of hearing founders saying..."), admitting he was wrong.
- **Recurring formats:**
  - **Podcast notes** (430 to 520 words): hook, 3 to 5 bullet headlines, "My notes below with [guests]", then 4 or 5 numbered subheads written as questions or claims ("Why the Anthropic IPO Will Trade Flat"), each with a 2 to 3 sentence answer.
  - **Investment story** (65 to 180 words): result with a number, short backstory, a quoted line, a one-line lesson ("You will always underestimate the size of your winners."), then thanks and "LFG".
  - **Hot take** (45 to 135 words): blunt claim, short argument, a concrete instruction ("Commit today, 60 days, post one lesson per day").
- **Voice:** confident and absolute, one sentence per paragraph, numbers everywhere ($, x, %). Informal British slang ("banging", "stoked", "LFG"), mild swearing ("BS", "WTF"), self-promotional superlatives ("the best podcast you will listen to this week"), lots of tagged names. Older posts end with a hashtag block; recent posts (Oct to Nov 2026) don't.

**What underperformed:** video teasers and "blind ranking" videos (2,500 to 6,000 impressions), YouTube episode promos, jokes about current news (Jensen Huang posts: 1,700 to 5,600 impressions, around 1% engagement), "Who's at TC Disrupt? Let's meet" (3,236 impressions, 5 engagements) and link-only posts.

**By format** (median impressions; engagement rate = total engagement / total impressions): text of 15 words or fewer: 9,300 impressions, 3.5%. Longer text: 8,100, 2.7%. Quote posts: 6,300, 1.5%. - **Hooks:** one-line opinions about founders and ambition; "worst time in history to..."; "How founders think X works vs how it actually works"; "[Thing] I'd bet my life on:"; "If I had to start over today with no skills or experience..."; "I hit [result] in [time] and all I did was:"; colon cliffhangers that open a thread ("Here's every idea I tried before hitting $20M ARR:").
- **Structure:** mostly single posts with one sentence per line. Playbooks are 3 to 20 numbered points, split into a thread when long. His own numbers come back again and again as proof ("$20M run rate in 7 months", "25,000+ paying customers").
- **Length:** most posts are 5 to 50 words; the best reach came from posts of 10 to 12 words.
- **Voice:** hustle and ambition, confident and absolute. Often lowercase, CAPS for emphasis ("TASTE IS THE MOAT", "LOCK IN"), slang ("YNGMI"). Name-drops his mentor (Travis Kalanick) and his years of experience ("2 decades").

## What to borrow from each

Writing rules for Emile that blend the three references. Platform data so far: LinkedIn is Emile's main audience (about 10,000 followers versus 122 on X; see `results/2026-11-first-analysis.md`).

### Both platforms

1. **Lead with a result or a number** (Harry, Ben). Put the hardest fact first: "250 founders applied in less than a week", not "Last summer we ran...". This matches what already worked for Emile.
2. **Earn credibility from the ground** (Guillaume, Ben). Back each claim with something only Emile sees: Hexa House founders, events, real numbers. Reuse the same proof points across posts the way Ben repeats his run rate.
3. **One sentence per line, no hashtags** (all three).
4. **Let personality show sparingly:** one joke, coined phrase or French aside per post at most (Guillaume).
5. **Anchor every stance in a story or a number.** Blunt takes work for Harry and Ben, but Emile's own abstract contrarian posts underperformed so far.

### LinkedIn

6. **Long posts are a numbered list of 4 or 5 points.** Each subhead is a question or claim, followed by 2 to 3 sentences and one proof (Harry's notes, Guillaume's listicles). Use it for MOFU teardowns (out-of-home campaigns, outbound, product-led) and TOFU "what the best SF founders do".
7. **Short posts stay under about 120 words:** result, short story, then thanks or an ask (Harry's investment story, Guillaume's offer post). Use it for BOFU news.
8. **End every post with one clear ask** (Guillaume): apply, come Thursday, DM me. Tie it to the KPIs in `profile/goals.md` (inbound DMs, hacker house applications).
9. **Close `european-founders` posts with a Europe vs SF contrast line** (Guillaume).
10. **Run a recurring series** with a fixed name and structure (Harry's weekly notes, Guillaume's "part 1"). For example "Notes from the Hexa House". The name is an open question for Emile.

### X

11. **Default to a one-line opinion under 15 words** about founders, ambition or SF (Ben's best reach and engagement rate). Test it against Emile's current best X format, founder shoutouts as quote posts.
12. **Use "expectation vs reality" or "[N] things I'd bet my life on:" and end the first post on a colon** to open a thread (Ben's most-bookmarked posts). Good fit for MOFU topics (outbound, out-of-home campaigns).
13. **Tell milestones as a number staircase** ("$0 → $1M in 12 weeks / $1M → $10M in 10 weeks") with the proof attached (Ben's biggest post). Emile's equivalent: hacker house seasons, applications, YC acceptances.
14. **Write insider rules for outsiders** ("unspoken rules in Silicon Valley that Europeans miss"). It is Ben's best non-milestone format on Emile's core topic.
15. **Don't rely on video teasers, YouTube promos, news jokes or "who's at X, let's meet" posts:** they were Ben's weakest posts and match Emile's own weak off-pillar posts.
16. **Post more often.** Ben posts 2 to 3 times a day; Emile posted about 4 original posts a week. The target cadence is an open question.
