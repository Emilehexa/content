# Editorial line

How Emile sounds and what he talks about. The editorial line is a funnel with three pillars. Every post gets one `pillar` and one `topic` in its frontmatter.

Source: Emile's answers (2026-11-19).

## Positioning

> **Open question:** one sentence on what Emile wants to be known for, and for whom.

Public profile wording, for reference: "Growth @ Hexa" and "I do 0 to 1 GTM for ambitious early stage start-ups" (LinkedIn), "Leading the best European program in SF @Hexa" (X).

## Pillars

| Pillar | Funnel stage | Theme |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `tofu-us-entrepreneurship` | Top of funnel | Entrepreneurship in the US |
| `mofu-us-gtm` | Middle of funnel | GTM in the US (inbound, etc.) |
| `bofu-hexa-sf` | Bottom of funnel | What Hexa is doing in San Francisco |

### TOFU: `tofu-us-entrepreneurship`

| Topic | Sub-topic |
| --- | --- |
| `sf-founders` | What the best founders are doing in San Francisco |
| `european-founders` | Why European founders are better |
| `general` | Entrepreneurship in the US, outside the two sub-topics above |

### MOFU: `mofu-us-gtm`

| Topic | Sub-topic |
| --- | --- |
| `product-led` | Why you need to be product-led to succeed here |
| `ooh-campaigns` | Analyses of the best out-of-home campaigns |
| `outbound` | How to do outbound today |
| `general` | GTM in the US outside the sub-topics above (inbound, etc.) |

### BOFU: `bofu-hexa-sf`

| Topic | Sub-topic |
| --- | --- |
| `hexa-news` | News on Hexa |
| `hacker-house` | News on the hacker house |
| `hexa-events` | Events Hexa organises |

### Outside the funnel

`pillar: off-pillar` with `topic: other` for posts that fit no pillar (personal life, tools). `topic: unclear` when an imported post could not be classified from its text.

## Open questions

- **Tone of voice:** which 3 to 5 adjectives describe how Emile wants to sound?
- **Pillar mix:** what share of posts should go to each pillar?
- **Do's:** habits that make a post feel like Emile.
- **Don'ts:** topics, phrases, or styles to avoid.
- **Language:** English only, or French on some platforms? (His public profiles list English as professional and French as native.)
- **Examples to emulate:** posts, his own or others', that capture the right voice.
