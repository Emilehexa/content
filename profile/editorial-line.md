## Tone references

Two creators whose tone Emile likes (source: Emile's answers). The notes below come from their public LinkedIn posts, read on 2026-11-20 through a public scrape and web search.

### Guillaume Roux-Romestaing

[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/guillaumerx21/). Founder at Desk One, a16z scout, based in San Francisco, about 11,000 followers. Studied 13 of his posts from Apr to Nov 2026.

- **Hooks:** provocation ("If you're not a media company first, you're dead"), a money offer ("I want to send you $1k every month"), insider explainers ("How VC scout cheques really work"), a role-play opener ("POV: you just moved to SF as a European founder (part 1)"), a personal milestone ("8 months, and 1 AI Content Engine later - I now have 250,000 followers"), curiosity ("I've been wondering why so many great french founders are in Growth/GTM").
- **Structure:** two formats.
  - Short offer posts: hook, a few lines or a bullet list, then a direct call to action ("send us customers - we pay 10% rev-share").
  - Long listicles: a credibility line ("I scout startups for a16z"), then 4 or 5 numbered points with italic subheads ("*1. Sell it before you build it*"). Each point has a claim, a personal proof with names and numbers, and a takeaway. Ends on a Europe vs SF contrast ("Europe waits for you to be chosen. SF assumes you started last week.") and an ask ("add me on LinkedIn").
  - Bridge phrases: "Hear me out", "Here's how it actually runs", "Save this if...".
- **Length:** short posts 30 to 80 words; listicles 350 to 450 words.
- **Voice:** first person, casual, often lowercase, typos left in. Uses coined phrases ("trickle-down conte-nomics"), a repeated slogan ("attention is all you need"), absurd humour, credibility drops (a16z, YC, dollar amounts), tagged names, the occasional line in French and flag emojis. Topics overlap with Emile's: European founders in SF, GTM, founder advice.

### Harry Stebbings

[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/harrystebbings/). Founder of 20VC (podcast and venture fund), based in London, about 290,000 followers. Studied 13 of his posts from Oct 2025 to Nov 2026.

- **Hooks:** a bold claim with a percentage ("90% of the podcasts you hear on AI today are BS", "99% of them are former investment banker box tickers"), a result first ("36 hours after meeting Hugo Santos we agreed to invest $10M into Namespace."), an "uncomfortable truth" framing ("There are three ugly truths right now that we are not admitting."), a story teaser ("The story of Fractile is wild!"), frustration ("I am fed up of hearing founders saying..."), admitting he was wrong.
- **Recurring formats:**
  - **Podcast notes** (430 to 520 words): hook, 3 to 5 bullet headlines, "My notes below with [guests]", then 4 or 5 numbered subheads written as questions or claims ("Why the Anthropic IPO Will Trade Flat"), each with a 2 to 3 sentence answer.
  - **Investment story** (65 to 180 words): result with a number, short backstory, a quoted line, a one-line lesson ("You will always underestimate the size of your winners."), then thanks and "LFG".
  - **Hot take** (45 to 135 words): blunt claim, short argument, a concrete instruction ("Commit today, 60 days, post one lesson per day").
- **Voice:** confident and absolute, one sentence per paragraph, numbers everywhere ($, x, %). Informal British slang ("banging", "stoked", "LFG"), mild swearing ("BS", "WTF"), self-promotional superlatives ("the best podcast you will listen to this week"), lots of tagged names. Older posts end with a hashtag block; recent posts (Oct to Nov 2026) don't.

## What to borrow from each

Writing rules for Emile that blend both references:

1. **Open with a result or a number** (Harry). Put the hardest fact first: "250 founders applied in less than a week", not "Last summer we ran...". This matches what already worked for Emile (see `results/2026-11-first-analysis.md`).
2. **Earn credibility from the ground** (Guillaume). Back each claim with something only Emile sees: Hexa House founders, events, real numbers and names. Name and tag the people involved (both).
3. **Long posts use a numbered 4 or 5 point list.** Each subhead is a question or claim, followed by 2 to 3 sentences and one proof (Harry's notes, Guillaume's listicles). Use it for MOFU teardowns (out-of-home campaigns, outbound, product-led) and TOFU "what the best SF founders do".
4. **Short posts stay under about 120 words.** Result, short story, then thanks or an ask (Harry's investment story, Guillaume's offer post). Use it for BOFU news.
5. **End every post with one clear ask** (Guillaume): apply, come Thursday, DM me. Tie it to the KPIs in `profile/goals.md` (inbound DMs, hacker house applications).
6. **Use the Europe vs SF contrast line** as a closer for `european-founders` posts (Guillaume).
7. **Run a recurring series** with a fixed name and structure (Harry's weekly notes, Guillaume's "part 1"). For example "Notes from the Hexa House". The name is an open question for Emile.
8. **One sentence per paragraph, no hashtags** (both, recent posts).
9. **Let personality show sparingly:** one joke, coined phrase, or French aside per post at most (Guillaume).
10. **Be careful with blunt "X% are BS" takes** (Harry). Emile's own abstract contrarian posts underperformed so far; when taking a stance, anchor it in a story or a number.
