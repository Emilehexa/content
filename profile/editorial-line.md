## Funnel and pillars

Use `funnel_stage` and `pillar` labels in the frontmatter of drafts and posts.

### Top of funnel (`tofu`): entrepreneurship in the US

Broad-reach content that attracts the audience.

| Pillar label | Theme |
| --- | --- |
| `us-entrepreneurship` | Entrepreneurship in the US in general |
| `sf-founders` | What the best founders are doing in San Francisco |
| `european-founders` | Why European founders are better |

### Middle of funnel (`mofu`): GTM in the US

Expertise content that builds authority.

| Pillar label | Theme |
| --- | --- |
| `us-gtm` | GTM in the US in general (inbound, etc.) |
| `product-led` | Why you need to be product-led to succeed here |
| `ooh-campaigns` | Analyses of the best out-of-home campaigns |
| `outbound` | How to do outbound today |

### Bottom of funnel (`bofu`): what Hexa is doing in San Francisco

Content that turns attention into applications, attendance, and trust in Hexa.

| Pillar label | Theme |
| --- | --- |
| `hexa-news` | Hexa news |
| `hacker-house` | Hexa hacker house news |
| `hexa-events` | Events Hexa organises |

### Outside the funnel

Use `funnel_stage: none` with `pillar: off-pillar` for posts that fit none of the above (personal life, tools, jokes). Use `pillar: unclear` when an imported post cannot be classified from its text alone.
