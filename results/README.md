# Results

Monthly reviews of post performance.

## How to review

1. At the end of each month, make sure every post from that month in `posts/` has its `metrics` filled in.
2. Copy `_monthly-review-template.md` to `YYYY-MM.md` (for example `2026-11.md`).
3. Compare posts against each other and against the targets in `profile/goals.md`.
4. Promote any learning you trust to `playbook/learnings.md`.

Judge posts against your own baseline per platform, not against other creators.
